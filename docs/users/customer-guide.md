# Tracecart customer guide

Tracecart helps you find what you need, even if you don't know its name, buy it or have it found for you, and get it delivered. You can also ship goods you already own.

This guide describes what the website does today. Where something isn't available yet, it says so.

## 1. Your account

You can browse and search without an account. You need one to buy, to ask us to find something, to request a shipment, and to follow your orders.

**To create one:** choose **Account** at the top of any page, then **Create an account**. Sign up with your email address. The same page lets you sign in later.

On your account page you can:

- change your name and phone number (the carrier may call that number at delivery);
- see your requests, quotes and freight requests;
- choose which emails you get (see [section 14](#14-emails-we-send-you));
- sign out.

## 2. Browsing the departments

Tracecart is organised in departments: Fashion, Electronics, Computers, Phones & Accessories, Home & Living, Beauty & Personal Care, Sports & Outdoors, Automotive, Industrial & Business, Tools & Hardware, Machinery, Agriculture, Health & Medical, Office & School, and Toys & Games. Each department has categories, and some categories have their own subcategories.

**On a computer:** choose **All departments** under the search bar. Point at a department to see its categories, then choose one. The bar beside it also lists every department.

**On a phone:** tap the menu button (three lines, top left). Tap a department to open its categories, or **See all** to see everything in it.

**A department with nothing in it yet** says so. Sellers are still adding products. Choose **Find it for me** and we'll look for what you need (see [section 12](#12-find-it-for-me)).

**In a category,** use **Filter and sort** to narrow the list: brand, price, condition, where it ships from, and the details that matter for that kind of product (size and colour for clothing, memory for a laptop, voltage for a drill). Each filter you apply is shown, and you can remove it.

## 3. Searching

Type what you're looking for in the search bar at the top of any page. You don't need the exact name: words in French or English both work, and small spelling mistakes are corrected ("perseuse" finds "perceuse").

- **Suggestions** appear as you type: products, categories and brands. Use the arrow keys or tap one.
- **To search one department,** choose it in the list on the left of the search bar (on a computer).
- **If nothing matches,** the page says so and suggests searching with a photo or asking us to find it.

## 4. Searching with a photo

Choose the camera button beside the search bar (it says **Photo** on wide screens). You can then:

- add a photo, or a short video, of the item;
- describe what you need in your own words;
- or both.

Tracecart reads the photo and your description, works out what kind of product it is and its important details, and shows the closest products in the catalogue, with the reasons each one matches. You can then narrow the results with the suggested refinements.

What it doesn't do yet: tell you for certain that a part fits something you own. When fit matters (a car part, a charger, a spare part), check the details on the product page or ask us.

If the search service is unavailable, the page says so and searches your words only.

## 5. Reading a product page

A product page shows:

- the photos;
- the price, and the earlier price struck through when the price was lowered;
- **when a deal ends,** if the price is a deal;
- whether it's in stock near you, ships from a seller, or is ordered from abroad, and roughly when it would be handed over;
- who sells it: **Sold by Tracecart** or the seller's shop name;
- other offers for the same product, when there are several;
- quantity prices, when buying more costs less per unit;
- the specifications, references (Tracecart reference, manufacturer part number, barcode) and what's in the box;
- published reviews (with their photos) and the seller's answers, and published questions with their answers.

**Can't buy it here?** The page offers **Can't find what you need? Ask us for it**, which starts a "Find it for me" request with the product already named.

## 6. Prices and currencies

The price on a product is the item's price. **The full delivered price** (delivery, any customs duties, import taxes and clearance fees) is worked out at checkout for your address, **before you pay**. Nothing more is charged at the door.

**Seeing prices in another currency:** on a computer, choose a currency in **Show prices in** at the top of the page (on a phone, it's in the menu). Prices then also show an approximate amount, for example "≈ 68.60 EUR", next to the real price. It's only a guide, based on a rate we keep: you're charged in the store's currency (XOF today). Only currencies we keep a rate for are offered.

## 7. Deals, coupons and gift cards

- **Deals** are lower prices on some products for a limited time. See them all under **Deals** in the header. A deal's end date is shown on the product. If a deal ends while the item is in your cart, the cart tells you the price changed before you pay.
- **Coupons** are codes that take an amount or a percentage off the items (not off delivery or taxes). Type the code in **Coupon code** at checkout and choose **Apply**. If it can't be used, checkout says why (expired, already used, minimum not reached…).
- **Gift cards** pay for part or all of an order. Type the 16-character code in **Gift card** at checkout and choose **Use**. Checkout shows how much the card pays and what's left to pay. If the card covers everything, there's nothing else to choose. A card that pays only part of an order can't be combined with cash on delivery. If an order is cancelled, the card's part goes back on the card.

## 8. Your cart and checkout

1. On a product page, choose the quantity and **Add to cart**. The cart keeps your items, even if you haven't signed in yet, and joins your account's cart when you sign in.
2. In the cart you can change quantities, remove items, or **Save for later**. If an item's price or stock changed, the cart tells you.
3. Choose **Check out** and sign in.
4. Choose **To an address** (add an address with a landmark the driver can find) or **To a pickup point** (choose the country and the point, and give a phone number for the collection code).
5. Checkout shows each shipment, the delivered total, and the dates it should arrive between.
6. Choose how to pay. Only methods that can take this order are offered; when one can't, it says why. Methods offered today:
   - **Mobile money or card** (online, through CinetPay) where it's switched on;
   - **Cash on delivery**, where it's available and up to a limit;
   - **Bank transfer**: the order waits for your transfer, and the items are held for you meanwhile;
   - **Net 30**, for approved company accounts.
7. Choose **Place the order**. If the total changed since it was shown, checkout tells you and shows the new one before anything is placed.

## 9. Your orders and deliveries

Open **Orders** at the top of the page to see your orders. An order page shows:

- its status (waiting for payment, confirmed, being prepared, on its way, delivered…) and each step with its time;
- each parcel and where it is, from the events the warehouse, carrier and pickup point record (there is no live map);
- **your collection code** when a parcel is out for delivery or waiting at the pickup point: show it to the driver or the point;
- the payment, and the invoice (PDF) once the order is confirmed.

You can **cancel** an order that hasn't been paid or prepared yet. Once paid, ask us: we cancel it and refund you.

## 10. Returns and refunds

From a delivered order, choose **Send something back**, pick the items and say why. You can do this within the return window shown on the order (14 days after delivery by default).

1. We accept or refuse the request and tell you by email.
2. If accepted, send the item back and give the carrier's or drop-off reference.
3. We receive and check it, then decide: a refund, a replacement or a repair.
4. A refund is paid to you and a credit note (PDF) appears on the order. A gift card's part goes back on the card.

## 11. Reviews and questions

- **Reviews:** once a product has been delivered to you, its page lets you write a review (stars, an optional title and a few lines). You can add up to four photos while it waits. A person checks every review before it's published. A seller may answer your review; their answer is checked too.
- **Questions:** anyone signed in can ask a question on a product page. It's checked before it's published, and we may answer it.

## 12. "Find it for me"

When you can't find something, or don't know what it's called, choose **Find it for me** (in the header, on an empty page, or **Ask us for it** on a product page).

1. Describe what you need, add photos or a video, and say where it should be delivered.
2. Our team looks for it and sends you a **quote**: the item, delivery, any customs costs, and our fee, with the dates.
3. Accept the quote to place the order, then pay as at checkout.

A quote is not an order until you accept it. It's valid until the date shown on it.

## 13. Shipping something you already have

Choose **Ship something** in the header.

1. Say where the goods are and where they're going, describe them, and add photos or a video. If you don't know the weight or the size, say so: we estimate them, and the estimate is marked as one.
2. You get an **estimate** at once (cargo and an indicative price), then our **firm quote**.
3. Accept the firm quote and pay. We arrange the pickup, the transport and, where needed, customs. Follow it on the request's page.

## 14. Emails we send you

We always email what you need to buy and receive:

- the order confirmation, payment, a cancellation;
- your collection code, and a failed delivery attempt;
- returns, refunds and quotes.

On your account page, under **Emails**, you can choose:

- **Order progress** (on unless you turn it off): shipped, delivered, a delay or a new delivery date.
- **Deals and news** (off unless you tick it). We don't send these yet; ticking it records your agreement for when we do.

Emails you can turn off have a link to these settings at the bottom.
